06 / Independently developed concept

Building an AI-ready workforce.

A hypothetical capability strategy for a technology-enabled professional services organisation. This is not an internal employer plan or work delivered for Morae.

Concept project · not commissioned work
DAYS 01–30

Diagnose

Meet stakeholders, map role-specific needs and define what improved on-the-job performance should look like.

  • Stakeholder map
  • Skill baseline
  • Priority use cases
DAYS 31–60

Design

Develop targeted learning, realistic practice scenarios and performance support with safeguards.

  • Learning pathway
  • Hands-on practice
  • Manager support
DAYS 61–90

Pilot and evaluate

Test a defined learning intervention, gather data and identify improvements before scaling.

  • Feedback and observation
  • Performance signals
  • Scale decision
Measure capability, not just attendance.

Example evaluation signals: assessment performance, safe application in work samples, manager observations, adoption quality, error rates and time to proficiency. All targets require real baseline data.

Public context: Morae's website describes AI combined with human expertise, legal technology and information governance. The concept uses these themes only as a publicly informed thought exercise.
