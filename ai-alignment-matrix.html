PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT

Four criteria for real-world prompt quality.

01Goal

The intended task and useful outcome are clear.

02Context

Relevant audience and non-sensitive background are provided.

03Boundaries

Constraints, data controls and verification expectations are included.

04Format

The requested structure and quality conditions are specified.
