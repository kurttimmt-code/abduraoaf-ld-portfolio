MODULE 05 · 5–6 MIN

Scenario lab: a client-ready update

You are part of a fictional professional-services team preparing a short update for an external client. A colleague suggests using AI to speed up the draft.

YOUR GOAL

Produce a useful, defensible update — without compromising confidentiality or accuracy.

Make three decisions as the project moves from input → draft → approval. Each choice unlocks a piece of targeted feedback. If you choose a less appropriate response, you can try again.
