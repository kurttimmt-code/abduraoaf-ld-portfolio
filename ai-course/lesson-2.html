MODULE 02 · 5–6 MIN

Find the right work for AI

Before opening a tool, ask a better question: what work could AI support safely and usefully? Different tasks carry different levels of risk.

LOWER RISKGood starting points
  • Brainstorm public-facing workshop titles.
  • Turn your own non-sensitive notes into a checklist.
  • Rewrite a generic paragraph for clarity.
CONDITIONALUse with safeguards
  • Draft internal guidance using an approved AI tool.
  • Summarise approved documents within approved controls.
  • Prepare material requiring expert review.
HIGHER RISKEscalate / avoid
  • Upload confidential client files into an unapproved tool.
  • Make hiring or legal decisions without authorised review.
  • Send AI-generated facts to clients unverified.

The five-question readiness check

VValue — Will the draft save time or improve thinking?
AApproved — Is the tool and this type of use permitted?
LLeast data — Can I use anonymised or public information?
IInspect — Can I verify the result before use?
DDecision owner — Who is accountable for the outcome?
PRACTICAL DECISION

Imagine a workplace request

“Please ask a public chatbot to summarise the attached client contract and send the result to the client this afternoon.”

The need is real, but the workflow is unsafe without an approved tool, information-handling permission and substantive human review. Choose a permitted workflow instead.
