MODULE 03 · 6–8 MIN

Prompting is a design skill

A strong prompt is a small specification. It gives the AI enough detail to attempt a useful answer while making the expected outcome clear.

1. OutcomeWhat should the AI create?
2. ContextWho is it for and what background matters?
3. ConstraintsWhat must it avoid or include?
4. FormatHow should the result be organised?
5. CheckWhat should a human verify?
WEAK PROMPT

“Make a training plan about AI.”

MORE USEFUL PROMPT

“Draft a 20-minute introduction to generative AI for new employees. Include three measurable objectives, one low-risk workplace scenario and four knowledge-check questions. Use plain English. Do not invent company policies. Label anything requiring policy confirmation.”

Try the prompt builder

Build a first draft for a fictional, non-confidential workplace task. The fields assemble text on your device; they do not contact an AI service.

Select “Build my prompt” to see your structured instruction.

AFTER GENERATING

Review the first draft against the goal, provide corrective feedback, iterate, and check all facts and sensitive details before using it.
