01. AI AT WORK
Welcome to AI at Work. In the next minute, you will see how to use generative AI productively and responsibly in a professional environment.

02. WHAT IS GENERATIVE AI?
Generative AI can help draft text, suggest ideas, summarise material, or structure information. It predicts useful responses, but it does not guarantee factual accuracy.

03. AI IS AN ASSISTANT
A good use case is preparing a first draft of a meeting agenda, a training outline, or a message using information you are allowed to share.

04. GOOD INPUTS MATTER
Instead of saying write an email, explain the audience, purpose, tone, and required format. Clear instructions improve the usefulness of the draft.

05. PAUSE BEFORE YOU PASTE
Do not enter confidential client information, personal records, or restricted documents into unapproved AI tools. Follow your organisation’s data and technology policies.

06. CHECK BEFORE YOU SHARE
A polished answer may still contain errors, invented references, or unsuitable advice. Check meaningful claims against reliable sources and keep human approval in the workflow.

07. THE HUMAN STAYS ACCOUNTABLE
Use the model to support judgment, not replace it. Be especially careful with legal, financial, or other consequential decisions. Escalate uncertain cases.

08. READY TO PRACTISE?
Now continue the course. Decide when to use AI, practise a stronger prompt, and apply responsible review to a realistic workplace scenario.
