What is generative AI?

Generative AI creates new content such as text, images, audio or code from patterns learned during training. You give the system an instruction (a prompt); it generates a response based on that instruction and the context it can access.

THE CORE IDEA

Generative AI is helpful at producing a first draft. It does not automatically know whether that draft is true, current, authorised or appropriate.

⌕

Search and retrieval

Finds or retrieves information from available sources. It may help you inspect original material, though search results still require evaluation.

✦

Generative AI

Produces a new response: a summary, draft, table or suggested plan. Even fluent answers may contain invented information.

Three things to remember

01

Fluency is not evidence

A confident tone, citation-looking text or precise number can still be wrong. Verify significant claims against dependable source material.

02

Context changes quality

The more clearly you explain the task, audience, constraints and approved sources, the more useful the first draft may be.

03

People remain accountable

An employee, not the model, must judge whether output is accurate, safe and fit for its intended audience.

WORKPLACE EXAMPLE

Prepare a meeting agenda

A team lead asks an approved AI tool to suggest an agenda for a non-confidential team planning meeting. It drafts a practical structure in seconds. The lead still checks priorities, names and timing before sending it.
