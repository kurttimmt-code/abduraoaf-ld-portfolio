MODULE 04 · 5–6 MIN

Trust requires deliberate checks

AI can accelerate writing and analysis, but it can also invent facts, omit important nuance and reproduce biased patterns. In professional settings, human review is part of the workflow, not an optional final glance.

01

Protect inputs

Check tool approval and data-handling rules before entering client, employee or commercially sensitive information.

02

Check outputs

Confirm names, dates, figures, citations and other material claims against trusted primary sources.

03

Approve use

Ensure appropriate expertise, fairness, tone, transparency and an accountable human sign-off.

Never treat a generated citation as proof

A system may produce believable references that do not exist, or accurately name a source while misrepresenting what it says. Open the original material. Check whether the claim is supported. For legal, financial or regulatory decisions, follow the organisation's review requirements and obtain specialist input.

Information protection matters.

Do not assume a tool is approved just because it is technically accessible. Permissions, privacy controls, retention settings and contractual terms vary by provider and organisational configuration.
