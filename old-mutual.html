04 / Professional case study · Old Mutual

Learning for change and role readiness.

Learning Consultant | August 2023–September 2024. Supported onboarding and change learning for Old Mutual Finance, a division with more than 3,000 employees.

Start with performance, not course format.

The CV describes stakeholder consultation, analysis of capability needs, development of learning interventions and evaluation of adoption and post-training results.

Review note: The four-step sequence is a proposed storytelling structure. The exact project, timeline, personal contribution and results still require confirmation.
01 / DISCOVER

Consult and diagnose

Identify current and future capability requirements with relevant stakeholders and subject matter experts.

02 / DESIGN

Connect learning to business needs

Develop onboarding, change and role-readiness support appropriate to a regulated environment.

03 / ENABLE

Support transfer

Make it easier for learners to apply the knowledge or processes in their daily work.

04 / EVALUATE

Assess adoption and results

Use post-training evidence to understand effectiveness and identify opportunities for improvement.
